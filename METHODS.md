## 1. Questions and hypotheses (as revised)
- **Q1 Value & policy** — H1 above; also: retention makes the optimal rule more aggressive than a fixed-confidence rule.
- **Q2 Behavior** — H2 above; expected: under-use early with two tokens, thresholds falling too far in the 9th relative to MTV, material team heterogeneity (between-team SD of capture ratio > 0.10 after shrinkage), and residuals (p̂ − p*) moving toward zero over the season within player/team.
- **Q3 Perception (Tier 2)** — H3; individual challenge skill has low reliability (variance-components signal share < 0.3); role differences partly reflect team eligibility policy, so H3 is stated *conditional on being allowed to challenge*.
- **Q4 Umpires (Tier 2)** — the count-bias contrast at fixed d shrinks 2026 vs 2025 beyond the 2024→2025 placebo; an overturn causes a measurable zone correction by the same umpire on the next 20 called pitches (RD).
- **Q5 Framing (Tier 2)** — framing runs concentrate within ~1.5 in of the ABS edge; the top-to-bottom framing spread narrows vs 2025.
- **Q6 Rule design (Tier 2)** — three counterfactuals reported as miscalls corrected per game vs seconds added.

## 3. Ground truth: reconstructing the ABS zone
1. **Primary variant (pre-registered, not chosen post hoc):** any-part-of-ball, plate-midpoint plane, published height rule (27%–53.5% of batter height). **2026 Statcast:** Savant reports plate_x/plate_z on the mid-plate plane and sz_top/sz_bot as the ABS-defined edges from 2026 (Savant CSV documentation), so no propagation is applied and the certified height is implied by sz_top/0.535 (cross-checked against sz_bot/0.27 and roster height). **2015–2025 Statcast** (front-of-plate plate_x/plate_z): position anchored on plate_x/plate_z; the midpoint location adds only the 9-parameter increment x_mid = plate_x + [x_9P(y=8.5/12) − x_9P(y=17/12)] (same for z). Diagnostic: distribution of x_9P(front) − plate_x by pitch type. StatsAPI pX/pZ remain front-of-plate in 2026 and are treated accordingly.
2. **Signed miss d̂** in inches (negative inside). **Measurement model:** true ABS miss d* = d̂ + η, η ~ N(0, σ_meas²), heteroskedastic by edge type (top/bottom carry height error; sides do not); σ_meas estimated from challenged pitches (probit of overturn on d̂ by edge type). All downstream analyses use |d̂| < 0.5 in as a coin-flip band (sensitivity 0.3, 0.75).
3. **Validation and go/no-go:** agreement between our classification and the ABS verdict on 2026 challenged pitches, reported for {any-part, center} × {midpoint, front}. If the primary variant agrees on < 90% of overturn outcomes outside the coin-flip band, we switch to a **probabilistic strike model** P(ABS strike | x, z, height) fit on challenge outcomes and carry that uncertainty through §5–§7 (the paper says so explicitly).
4. **Heights:** for 2026 the certified height implied by Savant's ABS-defined sz_top/sz_bot is primary; for pre-2026 seasons roster height is the prior and a per-batter effective-height offset is estimated by maximum likelihood from vertical-edge outcomes (and Triple-A full-ABS calls where available), shrunk hierarchically; roster-only results are a sensitivity.
5. Secondary check: agreement with Savant's in-zone flags where exposed.

## 4. Pricing a call: WP model
- Estimand: population-average value of a flipped call from the challenging team's perspective, in WP.
- **Primary WP variant (v0.3): count-composed.** WP(S, b, s) = Σ_T P(T | b, s, bases, outs) · WP_base(S ⊕ T), where T is the plate-appearance transition (Δouts, bases after the PA, runs), P(T | ·) is the empirical distribution among Retrosheet 2021–2025 PAs that passed through count (b, s) from that base-out state, and WP_base(S) is a count-free model fit on 2015–2025 PA-start states (2020 excluded; monotone in score difference). Terminal counts (4, s) = walk and (b, 3) = strikeout are on the grid, so a flipped call's value includes ball-four/strike-three transitions, the inning-ending transition, walk-offs, and the extra-innings automatic runner. Robustness variant: the direct fit below.
- Direct-fit variant: WP(S, count) = P(home wins | inning, half, outs, bases, score diff, balls, strikes) fit on 2015–2025 pitch states (2020 and 7-inning DH excluded; extra innings 2021–25 with the automatic-runner base state), monotone in score difference; validated against an independent single-season empirical table (2024: r = 0.994, weighted MAE 2.5 pp) and Savant home_win_exp; robustness covariate: batter/pitcher quality (season wOBA/xwOBA, K/BB tendencies).
- **Value of a flip** at (S, b, s): terminal transitions listed explicitly — ball-four walk with forced-runner advancement; strike-three out with the inning-ending transition; runner handling after an overturn per the 2026 operations rules (opportunities tagged "PA-ending" so the dump index can be split). Leverage from the same table.

## 5. Perception and decision (two tiers)
- **Tier 1 (descriptive, headline):** P(challenge | d̂ bin, role, inning, tokens, leverage) and P(overturn | challenged, d̂ bin, role), with game-clustered CIs; role curves compared *within teams that allow both roles*, and separately by call direction (called-strike vs called-ball opportunities).
- **Tier 2 (structural):** the eligible challenger perceives m = d* + ε, ε ~ N(0, σ²_role,region); challenge iff a hurdle π_role,team is passed and m > τ(inning, tokens, leverage, count); posterior success probability p(m) = ∫1[d* > 0] f(d* | call, region) φ((m − d*)/σ) dd* / ∫ f φ, with the prior f estimated from all 2026 called pitches (this is also the arrival model). Likelihood on all eligible called pitches with outcomes {not challenged, challenged-upheld, challenged-overturned}, measurement error σ_meas from §3.2 inside the likelihood. **Pre-registered simulation-recovery test** (simulate from known σ, τ, π, σ_meas; recover) must pass before Tier 2 is reported. Reliability of individual skill via a hierarchical variance-components model (player and team random effects on propensity and accuracy using all called pitches), not split-half correlations.

## 6. Decision model: finite-horizon DP with a renewable token
- **State** x = (half-inning h, outs, bases, score diff, count, own tokens t ∈ {0,1,2}); opponent tokens t_opp treated as independent in the primary solution and included (3×3) as a robustness solve, reporting the difference.
- **Value** V(x, t) = expected future WP gain from optimal use of tokens relative to holding none. At an opportunity with success posterior p and states x⁺ (call overturned) and x⁻ (call stands): challenge iff p·[g + V(x⁺, t)] + (1−p)·V(x⁻, t−1) > V(x⁻, t), where g = WP(x⁺) − WP(x⁻) for the challenger. With MTV(x) ≡ V(x⁻, t) − V(x⁻, t−1): **challenge iff p·g > (1−p)·MTV − p·[V(x⁺,t) − V(x⁻,t)]**; the last term is second-order (one call rarely changes the future opportunity distribution materially): the primary DP evaluates the continuation on the realized stream and drops it, the challenge card drops it, and its magnitude is bounded in a robustness check that re-solves with continuation values conditional on the flipped state (score/base-out bucket after the flip).
- **Rules:** retention keeps t on success, t−1 on failure; the extras rule (a team with no challenges receives one at the start of each extra inning) coded per the 2026 rule text with the alternative reading pre-registered as a sensitivity.
- **Solver (v0.3):** state-based policy iteration on the empirical streams — a decision may use only (half-inning, score bucket at the pitch, outs, tokens) and the opportunity's own (g, p); each iteration evaluates that rule on every instance (expected value over success, continuation along the realized path, game-over continuation = 0) and re-averages continuation values by state; iterate to a fixed point. The sample-path backward max (v0.1 code) is retained only as a reference because it embeds within-half-inning foresight and overstates value.
- **Arrivals:** empirical half-inning opportunity streams (count, outs, bases, score, d̂, side) from 2026 (challenge era) with 2023–25 streams as the pre-challenge bound; backward induction over half-innings × score buckets × tokens with Monte-Carlo expectations; decision-time continuation values recorded by outs.
- **Outputs (primary):** the **challenge card** — break-even success probability p*(inning band × tokens × leverage band × count class), ≤ 8 cells in plain language, with the WP cost of the card vs the full DP; MTV in WP per game, wins per 162 games, and dollars at a pre-registered $/win (source stated); the inches translation m* = σ·Φ⁻¹(MTV/(g+MTV)) in an appendix; policy values for optimal, information-constrained optimum, observed, and named heuristics ("≥50% sure", "never before the 7th", FanGraphs count rules).

## 7. Actual vs optimal
- **Three benchmarks:** oracle (sees d*), information-constrained optimum (sees m with σ̂_role, applies p*, averaged over ≥1,000 ε draws), observed. **Capture ratio** = observed ÷ information-constrained optimum (both net of "never challenge"); oracle ÷ information-constrained = the perception cost. Failed challenges valued at −MTV(x,t). Shortfall decomposed into threshold error, missed opportunities, and role assignment (within call direction).
- **Dump index (WP-weighted):** Σ over 9th/extra-inning challenges of max(0, (p* − p̂)(g + MTV)), by score state (tie / one run / 2–3 / 4+) and half; **model-free test:** overturn rate and |d̂| distribution by tokens-remaining at fixed leverage and count, situation-matched to earlier innings; "dump" is declared only if overturn rate falls with tokens-remaining at fixed leverage.
- **Learning:** within-player and within-team event-time regressions of the residual (p̂ − p*) on games since first eligible appearance, month FE, playoff-odds bucket, arrival prior fixed at its April estimate. Secondary: thresholds by contract status and by PA-ending vs count-changing flips at equal g (individual incentives).

## 8. Umpire response (Tier 2)
- **Primary estimand:** change in the count-bias contrast — P(strike | d = {−1, 0, +1} in, 3-ball) − P(strike | same d, 2-strike) evaluated on the fitted called-strike GAM surface — 2026 vs 2025, with 2024→2025 as the placebo pair (the 2025 evaluation-buffer cut from 2 in to 0.75 in and the 2026 zone redefinition are level shifts to be reported, not absorbed). Zone-geometry movement (50% contour toward the ABS box: top, bottom, width) reported as its own result; borderline defined relative to each umpire-season's own contour for the bias constructs.
- **Within-game causal design:** sharp RD in d̂ among challenged pitches (donut = coin-flip band; CCT bandwidth): outcome = the same umpire's accuracy/zone shift over the next 20 called pitches; the discontinuity at d̂ = 0 identifies the effect of an overturn holding challenge selection fixed.
- **Exploratory:** 2025 spring ABS parks vs non-ABS parks (umpires who worked both, park-cluster FE); Triple-A 2023–24 rotation only where the rule-version table documents geometry.

## 9. Framing under challenges (Tier 2)
Framing runs 2023–2026 (GAM-residual method on `call_original`) decomposed by |d̂| bins; share of framing value on pitches beyond the batter's break-even threshold; spread across catchers by season; dollar translation using a stated $/run.

## 10. Rule-design counterfactuals (Tier 2)
Three pre-named counterfactuals, each one bar (extra miscalls corrected per game vs seconds added, with the 2026 and 2023–25 arrival regimes as bounds): (i) three tokens with retention; (ii) two tokens without retention; (iii) dugout initiation with a 10-second delay. ∞-token reported only as a mechanical bound. Zone/height variants live in §3 sensitivity, not here. Lucas-critique caveat stated.

## 11. Inference and robustness
Per-hypothesis primary estimand, specification, and numeric threshold (§0–§1); full-pipeline game-clustered bootstrap (≥200 replicates; refit WP, §5, arrivals; re-solve DP; recompute τ*, MTV, capture ratio, §10) with a nested MC-error check (< 10% of bootstrap SE); split: fit arrivals/perception on games through July 31, evaluate August–September; multiverse over WP variant, zone variant, height source, coin-flip band, role pooling — all reported.
